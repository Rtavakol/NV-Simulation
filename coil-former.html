Print & assembly guide

  1. Confirm the dimensions. With the default 100 mm winding ID and 2 mm wall, the clear bore is 96 mm. The 8 mm radial winding build gives a 116 mm winding OD; the 3 mm retaining edge gives a 122 mm flange OD.
  2. Inspect one piece in your slicer. Check the displayed size, print-bed fit, wall generation and support needs. The suggested flange-down orientation can require supports beneath the upper flange; the preview does not simulate printing.
  3. Test the fit before a full print. Clearance depends on your printer and material. Print mating pieces or a locally cropped joint test and adjust the seam clearance. Rounded puzzle tabs and matching sockets align the flange edges; do not force a tight fit.
  4. Assemble before winding. Bring the pieces together with their axes aligned. The puzzle joints can be engaged by an axial sliding motion. Dry-fit all seams, then secure with a suitable adhesive or external nonmagnetic restraint. This prototype does not include pins, screw holes, mounting feet or wire entry slots.
  5. Prepare the winding surface. Smooth sharp edges and cover the barrel seams with a suitable thin insulating layer if needed, accounting for its thickness. Wind one continuous wire onto the assembled former; separately wound electrical sections are a different design.
  6. Check operating conditions separately. This is a geometric design prototype. Winding loads, joint strength, insulation and coil heating have not been qualified. Field, turn-count and electrical calculations will be added later.
