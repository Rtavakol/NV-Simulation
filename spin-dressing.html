Model and limitations

Positive γ convention; no relaxation. Midpoint piecewise-constant Hamiltonian evolution via norm-preserving Rodrigues rotations. Floquet frequency magnitude is extracted from the one-period rotation angle; it is folded into the first quasienergy zone. J₀(x) is a signed high-frequency approximation, not an exact finite-y result.
