About MagLab

From a physical model
to a visible response.

MagLab brings together interactive tools for exploring magnetic sensing, NV-center optics and spin dynamics. The simulations are designed to support learning, laboratory discussions and the exploration of experimental ideas.

Start with light generation and collection, study how magnetic fields appear in an NV spectrum, or explore spins driven by an oscillating field. Each simulator offers its own controls and visualizations.

Results follow the assumptions described in each simulation. Use those assumptions when comparing with experimental data.
